# Asset credits

## `paul.glb` — the character of Paul

Built from the **Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library** — https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox
(avatar `Assets/Avatars/Adults/Male_Adult_21`, and the motion-capture clips `m_idle_neutral_01`, `m_walk_neutral_01`,
`m_walk_slow_01`, `m_run_neutral_01`, `m_gestic_talk_neutral_01`, `m_crouch_idle`, `m_cheer_01`).

Changes made for this game (see `tools/hero/`): the robe was re-coloured to undyed linen and its modern buttons and
pocket removed, the shoes re-coloured as leather, the patterned head-cloth replaced with plain wool, textures reduced to
1024 px, clips trimmed, facial-bone tracks dropped, and everything packed into one glTF file in metres.
